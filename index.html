Clause IV

When terms disagree

Skills can line up while a number still doesn't — salary, start date, scope. When that happens, either side can raise a dispute on that specific term. It's filed with GenLayer's Internet Court: a random panel of AI validators reviews the two positions and returns a ruling, usually within minutes, without either side needing to agree first.

The verdict below is computed with a placeholder rule (splitting the difference) and stored for that role/profile pair. Swap raise_dispute() in app.py for a real call to GenLayer's Intelligent Contracts when you're ready to make the ruling itself binding.
